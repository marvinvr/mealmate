# MealMate Privacy Policy

MealMate is a client for your own, self-hosted Mealie server.

- **No data collection.** MealMate has no analytics, no tracking, no ads and no backend of its own. The developer receives no data from the app.
- **Your server only.** Recipes, meal plans, shopping lists and account data are exchanged only between the app and the Mealie server you enter. How that server handles data is up to its operator.
- **On your device.** Your login token is stored in the iOS Keychain; cached recipes and images stay on your device and are removed when you sign out.
- **Sign-in.** If you use "Sign in with …" (OIDC), the login happens with your server's identity provider in Apple's secure web sign-in sheet.
- **Purchases.** Optional tips and supporter subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. MealMate only learns from the App Store which purchases exist on your Apple Account, to show the thank-you and unlock app icons; the developer receives no personal or payment details.
- **Contact.** Questions or issues: https://github.com/marvinvr/mealmate/issues
